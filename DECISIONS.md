Each entry: what was decided, why, and its evidence status. "Verified" means checked by a test or measurement
described in `progress.md`; "Decided" means a design choice with no measurement behind it.

| 23 | GPU/CPU parity is judged on two different things. *Pointwise* equality is asserted over a short horizon, at the existing tolerances: 1e-3 when the state format is float32 (every run recorded here) and 1e-2 for the float16 fallback (`parityToleranceHalf` in `orbium-gpu.js`). Neither tolerance was changed. The horizon is: 1, 5 and 20 steps for every pattern, extended to the 100 and 200 step checkpoints for O2u only. *Long-term correctness* is judged by behavioural metrics only: values stay in [0,1], mass persistence (0.8 to 1.1 of the seed), shape correlation (at least 0.85), steady motion, GPU against CPU observables (mass within 1, centroid within 2 cells), grid-size independence, and no WebGL errors. **Long-term pointwise numerical equivalence between GPU and CPU is not claimed**, for any pattern. For O2b the 200-step pointwise check was removed (owner-approved) rather than loosened: measured on the reported AMD renderer, its error was 1.77e-4 at step 100 and 1.70e-3 at step 200 against the 1e-3 tolerance, while its observables agreed (worst mass difference 0.0033, worst centroid difference 0.0026 cells). Neither tolerance is changed; only the horizon is. | O2b is more sensitive than O2u (an ad hoc CPU comparison of the reference against a float32-accumulating variant gave 2.8e-4 at step 150 for O2b, 5.7e-5 to 9.0e-5 for O2u). The GPU error is larger than that float32 variant's (8.4e-5 at step 200), and the cause was not isolated (shader `exp()`, fused multiply-add, tap order, or the driver are all untested). | Decided; the numbers are Verified (`orbium-gpu.html`), the CPU comparison is ad hoc (script not in the repo) |
| 24 | Observatory readback: the state is copied to the CPU asynchronously. `AsyncReadback` (`src/gl/readback.js`) queues `readPixels` into a PIXEL_PACK_BUFFER and a fence; every animation frame then only asks whether the fence has signalled (as FrameFence does), and once it has, copies the finished data out with `getBufferSubData`. No call waits for the GPU to finish work. The final copy is synchronous CPU work on the main thread. Each request is tagged with the simulation generation and an epoch *at submission*, so a result describes the state it was taken from, however late it arrives. At most one request is in flight. Only READ_FRAMEBUFFER and PIXEL_PACK_BUFFER are bound, and both are restored to their previous bindings in `finally`, also when a call throws. Read format: RED/FLOAT when the framebuffer reports it (4 bytes per cell: 16 KiB at grid 64 to 1 MiB at 512), otherwise RGBA/FLOAT (16 bytes per cell); a float16 state reports RED/HALF_FLOAT and is read as RGBA/FLOAT. A failure (exception, or no signal within 600 polls) shows "unavailable"; the simulation keeps running. On context loss the readback is dropped without GL calls and recreated with the GPU resources. | The owner's requirement: no wait for the GPU in the frame path. Synchronous `readPixels` would wait for all queued GPU work; a GPU reduction would need new shaders. | Strategy as proposed in the Observatory plan. Behaviour Verified (gpu-parity: exact copies, tag at submission while the state moved 5 steps on, float32 and float16, bindings restored including on a thrown call, no GL error; ui-flow: lifecycle). Main-thread cost measured in the built-in browser on the AMD renderer with in-page probes (not in the repo), warm: `getBufferSubData` median 1.0 / 1.1 / 3.2 / 3.6 ms at grids 64 / 128 / 256 / 512 (2.2 to 8.5 ms at 512 with the GPU loaded); fence signalled after 1 to 2 frames, at most 6 seen. The first copy at grid 512 after page load took about 45 ms (cold); a new readback is created after every context restore. Frame-rate effect in an ordinary browser is unmeasured |
| 25 | Observatory metrics, named for what they compute (`src/simulation/observables.js`): **mass** = sum of all cell values; **active area** = number of cells whose stored value is strictly greater than the active threshold; **centroid** = per-axis circular mean of the values on the torus, from row and column sums, with its concentration R (0..1) per axis, shown only when mass is at least the minimum mass and R is at least the minimum concentration on both axes ("none (no mass)" / "not meaningful (spread out)" otherwise). A spread in cells, (n/2pi)sqrt(-2 ln R), is computed but not displayed. **Values (approved by the owner on 3 Nov 2026):** active threshold 0.01, minimum mass 1e-3, minimum concentration 0.5. | Reproducible in the repo (`tests/unit/observables.test.js` with the fixtures): the 0.01 boundary is strict on the stored float32 value; seed active areas 220 (O2u) and 205 (O2b) against 222 and 207 non-zero cells; empty and trace fields give "no mass"; uniform, nearly uniform and evenly split fields fall below R 0.5 while both seeds are above it; the spread is grid-independent (O2u at 64 and 128 within 0.05 cells); equal to the earlier per-cell centroid. From an ad hoc CPU script, not in the repo: in evolved O2u and O2b states only 2 to 5 cells lie in (0, 0.01], carrying at most 3.4e-4 of the mass; float16 rounding changed the active count by 0 and the mass by at most 8e-4; R is 0.93 (grid 64) to 0.98 (128) for the organisms, 0.82 for the noise disc, 0.00 for two copies half a grid apart; a fading organism keeps R >= 0.94 until its mass reaches exactly 0. | Definitions Decided; values approved by the owner (3 Nov 2026) on the evidence listed |
