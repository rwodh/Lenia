## Owner clarification (zoom above 1, straddling the edge)
Built as asked: a window that straddles the world's edge wraps seamlessly; minimum zoom is 1. Precisely: exactly one period of
the world is drawn around the view centre. On a non-square canvas at zoom 1 that is the original letterbox; as the zoom grows
the bars on the long axis shrink and are gone once the zoom reaches the canvas aspect ratio (e.g. 1.5 on a 3:2 canvas). They
never appear at the seam: bars only mark where the window is wider than the whole world, which avoids drawing the organism twice.

## Magnification
Stays bilinear (unchanged). Screenshot at zoom 8 on O2u (built-in browser, AMD renderer) was shown to the owner; the cells are
smoothed, not shown as squares. Proposal, not implemented: an optional "Pixels" toggle that switches the shader's
`sampleState` to a nearest-cell fetch, display only, default off.

positions; the app was not changed.
