- `src/ui/tour.js`: the tour selects O2u with `{ centred: true }` on open and on leaving (two call sites), so its experiment

- Docs: `DECISIONS.md` 29, `README.md`, `progress.md`.

## 4b: design summary (details in `docs/design/second-organism.md`)
GPU "stamp" pass (no state upload, one pass, wrap in the shader; the step shader untouched), combination rule `max`, copies
of the selected organism only (mu, sigma and T are global; O2b's verified sigma differs from O2u's), Reset removes additions,
Observatory keeps its history with a marker and discards the in-flight readback, locked during the tour. Two owner decisions
are listed at the end of the note.
