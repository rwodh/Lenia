## Owner review notes carried forward (not acted on here)
- In the built-in pane the loop was GPU-limited 89 to 99 % of the time even at grid 64; if ordinary browsers behave the same, the
  Observatory rarely samples. First thing to check after deployment.
- The ANGLE warning "READ-usage buffer was written, then fenced, but written again before being read back": first thought to be
  a discarded readback (Reset, switch, and now additions cancel one). Corrected on 8 Nov 2026 after a trace in Chrome on the same
  renderer: it follows each new Observatory readback request, also after the previous result was read, and stops when the
  readback is switched off, so it comes from the readback itself, not from discarded requests (Reported). Its cost is unmeasured.
- `role="application"` on the canvas needs an accessibility review before release.
- Repository hygiene: `.claude/settings.local.json` is ignored only by the owner's global Git ignore; `.vscode/` and
  `.claude/launch.json` are untracked. Adding them to `.gitignore` is the owner's call (not done here).
