# M2 report: floating, full-screen interface and periodic surround

Date: 9 Nov 2026. Branch `feature/floating-ui`, created from `fix/release-hardening` at `804e461` (M1). This report is committed
with the work it describes (see `git log feature/floating-ui`). The owner approved the layout from screenshots on 9 Nov 2026
before the tests and docs were finished. Nothing was pushed.

**M2: DONE.** Open decisions are listed at the end.

## What changed
- `index.html`, `styles.css`: the world fills the viewport; wordmark, status chip (state, generation, GPU-limited badge),
  Hide-UI button, two icon rails, six floating panels (Organism, View, Help on the left; Parameters, Observatory, Status on the
  right), the transport bar (bottom centre), the zoom tools with the cell readout (bottom right) and a note toast (top centre).
  Every id and `data-*` hook used by the tests and the tour is kept. Icons are inline SVG with presentation attributes only.
  Explanations (organism description, parameter meanings in English and Bangla, Observatory definitions) sit behind `<details>`
  disclosures; nothing was removed. Narrow screens (720 px and below): one bottom toolbar, bottom sheets, transport bar above it.
- `src/ui/panels.js` (new): open/close, one per side (one at all on narrow screens), Esc closes the latest and returns focus,
  close buttons, Hide-UI (`H` and the buttons). `src/ui/tooltips.js` (new): hover and keyboard-focus tooltips (WCAG 1.4.13).
- `src/ui/hud.js`: status chip, State-format row, dismissible note toast (a persistent note, such as a lost context, has no close
  button). `src/ui/controls.js`: Periodic surround On/Off and Panels Glass/Solid. `src/ui/tour.js`: opens the Parameters panel
  for its parameter steps and closes all panels on leaving; the steps, gates and experiment are unchanged. `src/main.js`: wiring;
  `window.leniaUi` under `?debug` for the tests.
- `src/shaders/display.frag.js`, `src/render/renderer.js`: the periodic surround (decision 35); the main square is drawn by the
  same code as before. `src/render/view-math.js`: `{ surround }` option for the pointer mapping. `src/app.js`: `surround`,
  `panelStyle`, their setters, the snapshot, the persistent lost-context note. `src/config.js`: `SURROUND`, `PANEL_STYLES`.
- Tests: `gpu-parity.js` (4 surround checks), `view-math.test.js` (1 test), `ui-flow.js` (a panel helper, the 390 px Observatory
  check now opens its panel first, 18 M2 checks, 4 tour checks; no check was removed or weakened).
- Docs: `DECISIONS.md` 35 and 36, `progress.md` (status rows, fact 26, open items), `README.md`, this report.

Protected files unchanged since M1 (step shader, kernel, CPU reference, RLE decoder, pattern data, fixtures, `FrameFence`,
`LIMITS`, tour experiment and gates); Verified with `git diff`.

## Contrast (text on the glass panel)
Panel `rgba(13, 18, 28, 0.80)` composited in sRGB over the brightest colour of each map (what blur averages towards in the worst
case, a uniform bright area); WCAG contrast ratio. Script: `contrast.js` in the review scratchpad, reproduced here.

| Background behind the panel | Composite | Text `#e7ecf6` | Muted `#b6c0d4` | Accent `#3fd6c8` | Amber `#ffc477` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| viridis(1) rgb(252, 231, 33) | rgb(60.8, 60.6, 29.0), L 0.0437 | 9.46 | 6.13 | 6.22 | 7.16 |
| Ocean(1) rgb(255, 217, 133) | rgb(61.4, 57.8, 49.0), L 0.0423 | 9.60 | 6.22 | 6.32 | 7.26 |
| white (worst possible) | rgb(61.4, 65.4, 73.4), L 0.0532 | 8.59 | 5.56 | 5.65 | 6.50 |
| Solid panel `#111826` | - | 14.99 | 9.71 | 9.86 | 11.34 |

The earlier muted grey `#94a0b9` would reach only 3.87 to 4.33 on glass, so muted text was lightened to `#b6c0d4`. Composite:
`C = 0.80 * panel + 0.20 * background` per sRGB channel; luminance from linearised channels; ratio `(L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05)`.

## Cost (Reported, scratchpad probe, 6 s per case)
| Case | 1440 x 900 | 1280 x 720 |
|---|---|---|
| Display pass GPU time p50 / p90, panels closed, surround on | 0.88 / 1.25 ms | 0.59 / 0.93 ms |
| Same, surround off | 0.80 / 1.01 ms | 0.38 / 0.71 ms |
| Frame interval p90, two glass panels open | 7.4 ms | 13.9 to 14.0 ms |
| Frame interval p90, two solid panels open | 7.4 ms | 7.4 ms |

The full-viewport display pass is cheap (about 1 ms or less). Blur's effect is inconclusive: at 1280 x 720 the glass runs showed
more 14 ms frames than solid, but one run without any panel showed the same; it happens in the browser's compositor, outside the
timer query. Glass stays the default; see the open decisions.

## Deviations and open decisions
- The tour card for docked steps sits at the top left on desktop (between the left rail and an open right panel) and at the top
  on phones, where a sheet then ends below it, so both the step and its control show. Not specified in the brief.
- Notes keep the `data-hud="note"` element inside the new toast, so existing checks still read it.
- Open: (1) the GPU-limited **flag rule** - decided and implemented 9 Nov 2026 as a deficit-plus-hold-back rule, see the M1
  report, Addendum 2 (144 Hz behaviour not yet re-measured);
  (2) default to Solid panels on devices that are really GPU-limited, once the flag is trustworthy; (3) remembering open panels
  per viewer (optional in the brief; not done, owner decision 5).
